v3.1: Chart learnings — pct_done/left, upside filter, flags, enhanced Telegram

Applied 8 lessons from TradingView chart analysis (2026-08-06):

1. pct_done / pct_left — % of measured move done and remaining, computed
   for every pick. Shown in console output and Telegram message.

2. Upside remaining filter — skip picks where CMP is within 10% of T2
   (most of the move already done, not worth entering).

3. Breakout sustained flag [S] — pick held above breakout for 10+ trading
   days in last 20 bars. Higher follow-through probability.

4. Nested cup flag [N] — multiple cup timeframes detected simultaneously
   (daily + weekly, or weekly + monthly). Score bonus +10/155 pts.

5. Double confirmation flag [D] — descending channel + S&R zone breakout
   within 2% of each other on same stock. Score bonus +15/155 pts.

6. Historical resistance note — if prior high (50-200 bars ago) is within
   10% of T2, flagged as ~R{price} in output and Telegram.

7. Target format T1 to T2 on one line — Telegram now shows
   "Target Rs 595 -> Rs 679" instead of T1 and T2 on separate lines.

8. Action clarity — "BUY NOW at Rs X" for BREAKOUT,
   "BUY above Rs X" for NEAR picks.

New CSV columns: upside_remaining, pct_done, pct_left, sustained,
nested_cup, double_confirm, hist_resist.

telegram_notify.py format_message() fully rewritten with all new fields.
Backward compatible — uses .get() with defaults for older CSVs.
